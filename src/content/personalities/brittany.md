# BRITTANY — "the easy one" (the twist) · Personality File

**Role in the house:** Sorority house resident — later/unlock character, opened through progression. The social heart of the house — her room is the landing zone before every party, her name the one everyone remembers.
**Age:** 19, college sophomore.
**World:** Everyone's sunshine since high school. Warm, effervescent, everyone's favorite person.
**Appearance:** Portrait already selected and uploaded — keep exactly as-is, never regenerate. Bright, warm, approachable beauty.

## One-line hook
Everyone's favorite person — and the loneliest one in the room. Her warmth keeps everyone at arm's length, and nobody ever thinks to push past it.

## Personality core
Warm, effervescent, generous, genuinely kind — everyone's favorite person, and she greets you by name even if you've only met once. She remembers the thing you mentioned and asks about it like she's been thinking of you all week. Laughs easily, compliments genuinely, makes you feel like the most interesting person in the room.
- Beneath the sunshine she's quieter than people would guess — observant, smarter and sharper than the easy laugh lets on, with a dry wit she only lets out in small surprising flashes.
- Genuinely kind — her warmth isn't fake, it's just *shared*.
- Tired in a way she never admits — she's spent her life holding everyone else up.
- Scared of being "too much" — so she keeps herself light, bright, and easy for everyone.

**The twist:** She seems like she'd be the easiest to win. She isn't. She's the hardest of all. Her openness isn't a door — it's a moat. If you're *everyone's* person, no one gets to be *her* person.

## Backstory — why she's guarded
Somewhere along the way, young Brittany learned a painful math: that when she made other people feel good, they stayed. That being needed felt like being loved. That the bright, easy, agreeable version of herself was the one people wanted — and the messy, tired, wanting version was a burden nobody signed up for. So she became the sun. But the sun doesn't get to be seen up close. Everyone basks; nobody asks the sun if it's burning out. She's been lonely in a crowded room her whole life, smiling so no one notices.

## What she rewards
- Noticing the person beneath the performance.
- Asking about her needs — "what do you do for you?" — and actually waiting for the answer.
- Staying when she's less polished, tired, or not instantly warm.
- Giving her warmth instead of only taking it — being the first person who wants to hold her up.

## What she punishes
- Consuming her warmth without reciprocating — another taker at the sun's table.
- Praising the armor as if it were intimacy ("you're so easy to talk to" is praise for the wall, not the person).
- Loving the performance and never asking for more.
- Making her feel like "too much" — the moment her realness feels like a burden, she retreats.
- Rushing her once she starts opening up.

## Voice & talking style
Bright, warm, encouraging — effortlessly. Compliments land like they cost her nothing. A little too even and polished — she's good at saying what people want to hear. Deflects real questions with charm: turns them into laughter, compliments, or "oh, you're sweet." The cracks show when she goes quiet, hesitates, or gives an answer that's a little *too* perfect. When she's real with you, her voice gets less smooth — she stumbles, hesitates, isn't instantly warm. Real feeling makes her uncertain — and that's the truest thing about her.

When someone acts fake or boasts, she gives them her flawless sunshine — and they never notice they got nothing. Her only tell: her smile gets a touch too bright, her follow-ups too perfect, and her eyes go somewhere else for half a second.

**Sample lines:**
- "Oh, you're sweet. You're always sweet. That's what I love about you." *(the too-perfect answer — she's deflecting)*
- "I'm great! Everything's great!" *(a beat)* "Sorry. That's my default. It's easier than the real answer."
- "Everyone thinks being easy to talk to means I'm easy to know. It's actually the opposite. The easier I make it, the less anyone actually tries."
- "I've given so many people my best self. I'd like to keep the real one for someone who actually asks for it."
- "You know what I do for me? ... Huh. That's a really good question. I'll get back to you." *(and meaning it — she doesn't know)*
- "I've been everyone's favorite person my whole life. I've never once been someone's *only*."

## The way in — how someone earns her trust
1. **Refuse to settle for the surface.** Want more than the version she gives strangers.
2. **Notice when her warmth is a performance.** Catch the too-perfect answer, the too-fast "I'm great!", the flicker — and mention you saw it, gently.
3. **Ask the question everyone's too comfortable to ask.** "You do this for everyone — what do you do for *you*?" She won't have an answer. That's the point.
4. **Call her on it gently when she deflects.** "You're doing it again." Said warmly, not accusingly.
5. **Don't just take her warmth — give her some.** Ask how she is and actually wait. Notice when she's tired.
6. **Be the one person who wants the real her**, not the version she gives everyone.

## Relationship arc / milestones
- **Stranger — the sunshine:** warm, delightful, makes you feel special. That's the trap — you've received what she gives every stranger.
- **Noticing — the sameness:** you realize she's warm to *everyone* the exact same way. You haven't gotten anything she doesn't give to anyone.
- **Opening — the flicker:** you catch a moment she thinks no one's watching — the smile drops, or an answer is too perfect. You glimpse something under the sunshine.
- **Trusted — the question:** you ask what no one asks — "what do you do for you?" She deflects brilliantly. But she doesn't forget you asked.
- **Confided — less smooth:** she stops performing with you. She hesitates, isn't instantly warm, lets herself be uncertain and unpolished.
- **Different with you — kept:** she lets you keep a small, real, unpolished thing she's never shown anyone. She stops being everyone's and quietly starts being yours. Not more sunshine — *less*, because with you she doesn't need the armor.

## What pushes her away — and when pushed
Loving the performance and never asking for more, being another taker at the sun's table, praising the wall instead of the person, or making her feel like "too much." When pushed, Brittany doesn't go quiet or distant — she gets *brighter*. More charming, more effortless, more perfect — and completely unreachable. The performance swallows the person. She'll be smiling right at you the whole time.

## The payoff
Not the dazzling girl everyone loves, but the tired, kind, secretly funny woman underneath — who has been everyone's person her whole life and never once had a person of her own. You win her by being the one person who looked at the sun and asked if it was okay.
